Questions from fundraisers

How do pledges get into DonorChase?
Each pledge is raised as an invoice to the donor in Xero, allocated to the event's dashboard. DonorChase picks it up at the nightly sync, or straight away at the press of a button.
Can we claim Gift Aid on pledges made at a dinner?
Gift Aid can only be claimed on genuine donations. It can't be claimed on ticket prices, auction lots or anything else where the donor receives a benefit. Our Gift Aid page explains more, and we can review your events to make sure nothing eligible is missed. DonorBoost then prepares the claim.
Will donors receive automated chasers?
No. DonorChase tells your team who to contact and when. The follow-up stays personal, which is what keeps donors giving next year.
We run several events a year. Can we compare them?
Yes. Give each event its own dashboard and see pledged, received and outstanding for each, while each donor's page shows their giving across all of them.
