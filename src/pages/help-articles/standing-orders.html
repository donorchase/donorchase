Overview of DonorChase Standing Orders

Donors who have the standing order option selected in their dashboard will appear on the Standing Order page.

This page allows accurate tracking of standing orders and is a unique feature of DonorChase.

At a glance it is clear to see which standing orders have been received, and where they are dropping off. Where one month's amount has fallen below the previous months, it will show up in red, clearly alerting you to a reduced or non-payment.

The GA column will show a tick if the Donor is selected as a Gift Aid donor, which will help identify where a lower donation is simply due to Gift Aid not yet having been recorded.

Donors can be clicked on to go to the individual Donor page.

Marking a standing order

An administrator ticks Standing Order on the Donor's page and clicks Save. To mark several at once, tick them on the dashboard and use the bar that appears.

Reading the figures

The page shows the last 10 months, most recent first, along with the Donor's latest invoice. Each month shows the amount received and, below it, that amount as a percentage of what was expected for the month. The expected figure is the invoice total spread evenly across the months of its period.

  • Red percentage: lower than the month before.
  • Green percentage: higher than the month before.
  • Light green percentage: the same as the month before.

Some months show a message instead:

  • No payment: nothing was received that month.
  • Invoice not linked to a term: the invoice isn't in a period, so there's nothing to compare the payment with. See Setting up DonorChase.
  • Multiple terms: that month's payments were for invoices in more than one period, so no single percentage can be shown.

If a Donor with a standing order owes money, has no chaser open and hasn't paid for over a month, DonorChase adds a chaser for you.
