DonorChase and Xero

DonorChase connects to Xero to show you, donor by donor, what has been invoiced, what has been paid and what is still owed, so your team knows exactly who to chase. You keep raising invoices and recording payments in Xero as you do today. DonorChase reads them and does the rest.

Read-onlyDonorChase never creates, changes or deletes anything in Xero.
Syncs nightlyPlus a Sync Now button whenever you want the latest figures.
Free for a monthSign up with Xero in minutes, with no card needed.
