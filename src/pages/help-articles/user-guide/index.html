DonorChase User Guide

DonorChase shows your team, donor by donor, what has been invoiced, what has been paid and what is still owed, then helps you chase it and keep a record of every conversation. Work through this guide in order if you're new, or jump to the part you need.

The guide

  1. Setting up DonorChase Periods, groups, dashboard settings, and putting new invoices on the right dashboard.
  2. How to operate DonorChase Finding your way around the dashboard and what each column means.
  3. Chasing donors Filters and colours, chasers, comments, automatic chasers and reminder emails.
  4. Individual donor pages Everything about one donor: statement, contacts, chasers and comments.
  5. Standing orders Seeing which standing orders have arrived, and which are dropping off.
  6. Multiple dashboards Keeping separate activities, such as fees and an appeal, apart.
  7. Your team and users Inviting people, administrators, assigning donors, and escalating to a second level.
  8. WhatsApp messages Drafting standard messages and sending them to a donor's contacts.
  9. Importing and exporting Uploading contacts and comments from a spreadsheet, and downloading a dashboard.
  10. Your login and account Logging in, text message codes, passwords, your profile and switching organisation.

Words DonorChase uses

WordWhat it means
SchoolDonorChase calls each organisation a school, whatever kind of organisation you are. You'll see this in a few places, such as Manage Schools.
DonorA contact from your bookkeeping package.
ContactA person you speak to about a donor, with a phone number and email. One donor can have several, for example both parents in a family.
DashboardThe main table of donors and what they owe. Many organisations need only one.
Period (or term)The stretch of time invoices are grouped into on a dashboard, such as a year or a school term.
GroupA section of a dashboard that sorts donors by how you deal with them, such as Standing orders or Do not chase. A donor is in one group per dashboard.
TagA label on a donor that you can filter by. A donor can have as many as you like.
ChaserA dated reminder to contact a donor. Once done, it's completed with a note of what happened.
CommentA note about a donor, with the date and who wrote it.
Team memberA DonorChase user. Each donor can have a Responsible Team Member who looks after them.
