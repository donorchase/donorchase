Questions from schools

Do we need to change how we use Xero?
Contributions need to be raised as invoices so DonorChase knows what's been requested and from whom. Many schools already do this with repeating invoices. Because raised invoices count as income before the money arrives, you may want to run reports on a cash basis, or post a journal for amounts not yet received. We can help with both.
Is it appropriate to chase voluntary contributions?
DonorChase isn't debt collection software. It's designed for polite, personal reminders to families who have chosen to contribute, at a time that suits them. It avoids terms like "dunning" and "arrears", and nothing is sent automatically to parents.
Can we still claim Gift Aid on voluntary contributions?
Gift Aid can only be claimed on genuinely voluntary gifts where the donor receives no benefit in return. DonorChase is there to remind families of what they've pledged, not to pressure them. See our Gift Aid page for more. DonorBoost, our Gift Aid claims software, prepares the claim itself.
Does it work across a multi-academy trust?
Yes. There's no limit on users or dashboards, so each school office can work its own list while the trust's finance team sees the whole picture. Talk to us about how best to set it up for your trust.
Can we keep school fees and after-school activities separate?
Yes. Multiple dashboards keep different kinds of income apart, for example contributions, fees and after-school clubs, while each family's page shows everything in one place.
