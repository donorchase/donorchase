Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how DonorChase collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this Website, contact us, or use the DonorChase software. DonorChase takes the privacy of your information very seriously. Please read this privacy policy carefully.

Definitions

In this privacy policy, the following definitions are used:

  • Data Protection Law: the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, as amended from time to time;
  • Service: the DonorChase donation tracking software, available at app.donorchase.com;
  • We or us: DonorChase;
  • You: any person whose personal data we handle, including visitors to the Website, people who contact us, and users of the Service; and
  • Website: www.donorchase.com and app.donorchase.com.

Who we are

DonorChase is provided by Chasm (Grants) Limited, trading as DonorChase, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 14190757, whose registered office is at 13 Elmcroft Avenue, London NW11 0RS. For the personal data described in this policy, Chasm (Grants) Limited is the controller under Data Protection Law. You can contact us about this policy or your personal data by email at info@donorchase.com.

Donor data held in the Service

Schools and charities use the Service to manage information about their donors, much of which is imported from their accounting software, such as Xero or QuickBooks. For that information, the school or charity is the controller and DonorChase acts as its processor, handling the data only on its instructions as set out in our Terms and Conditions. If you are a donor and have a question about your data, please contact the school or charity concerned. We will help them respond.

Personal data we collect

When you contact us

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, organisation, organisation type, which of our products you are interested in, bookkeeping package, your message, and whether you would like to join our mailing list. We also record which page of the Website you sent the enquiry from. If you email or phone us, we collect the details you give us.

When you use the Service

When a user account is created for you, we collect your name, email address and mobile phone number, and your organisation's link to the account. We store your password only in a securely hashed form. We also keep records of your logins, including when you logged in, so that we can keep accounts secure.

If you sign up with Xero, Xero shares your name and email address with us, and the organisations you choose, so that we can set up your account.

When your organisation subscribes, we collect the billing name, email address and address you give, and keep a record of your subscription and payments.

When you visit the Website

Our hosting providers may record technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in standard logs used for security and to diagnose problems.

How and why we use your personal data

Data Protection Law requires us to have a lawful basis for using your personal data. We use it:

  • to respond to your enquiry and, if you are interested, to discuss how DonorChase could help your organisation, because it is in our legitimate interests to do so, or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract;
  • to provide the Service, including sending login codes by text message, password reset emails, and the summary emails you choose to receive, because this is necessary to perform our contract with your organisation and in our legitimate interests;
  • to keep the Website and Service secure, and to diagnose problems, because it is in our legitimate interests to do so;
  • to send you news and updates about DonorChase by email, but only if you have opted in to our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any of these emails or by emailing info@donorchase.com; and
  • to comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who we share your personal data with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the Website and Service, and only as far as necessary. They act on our instructions and are required to keep it secure. They include:

  • GitHub, which hosts this Website (www.donorchase.com) and, like most web hosts, records visitors' IP addresses for security;
  • Formspree, which receives the enquiries sent through our contact form and passes them on to us by email;
  • Vultr, which hosts the Service (app.donorchase.com) and its data on servers in London;
  • Google, which provides our email service, used to receive enquiries and to send emails from the Service;
  • The SMS Works, which sends the login codes we text to users of the Service;
  • Stripe, which processes subscription payments. Card and bank details are entered directly with Stripe and are never seen or stored by DonorChase;
  • Xero and QuickBooks, where a school or charity connects its accounting software to the Service, so that the Service can read its accounting data.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, or to a prospective buyer if all or part of DonorChase is sold, in which case we will take steps to ensure your privacy continues to be protected.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may process personal data outside the UK. Where they do, we ensure the transfer is protected as Data Protection Law requires, for example because the country has been found to provide adequate protection, or by using the standard contractual clauses approved for this purpose.

How long we keep your personal data

  • Enquiries are kept for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer.
  • User account details are kept while the account is active, and deleted when it is removed or when your organisation's subscription ends.
  • Mailing list details are kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Donor data held in the Service is kept as instructed by the school or charity, and deleted at the end of its subscription, as set out in our Terms and Conditions.

Cookies

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary for the Website and Service to work, so they do not require your consent. www.donorchase.com sets no cookies. The Service sets a cookie that keeps you logged in, a cookie that remembers you have entered your login code on that browser, and short-lived cookies that protect signing in and signing up with Xero against forgery. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

When you subscribe or manage billing, you do so on Stripe's own pages, which use their own cookies, as described in Stripe's privacy policy.

Security

Data security is of great importance to DonorChase. We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and two-step verification by text message for users of the Service.

You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential. Transmission of information over the internet is never entirely secure, but we will do our best to protect your personal data.

Your rights

Under Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate personal data corrected;
  • have your personal data deleted, in some circumstances;
  • restrict or object to our use of your personal data, in some circumstances, and at any time where we use it for direct marketing;
  • receive your personal data in a portable format, in some circumstances; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@donorchase.com. There is normally no charge, and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please contact us first so that we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Links to other websites

This Website may, from time to time, provide links to other websites. We have no control over such websites and are not responsible for their content. This privacy policy does not extend to your use of such websites. You are advised to read the privacy policy of other websites before using them.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the law or in how we work. The current version will always be posted on this page, and if we make significant changes we will let users of the Service know by email.
