Who are we?

At DonorChase, we are a dedicated team with over a decade of experience in the charitable and educational sectors. Our passion lies in advancing the objectives of schools and charities by providing integrated, efficient, and easy-to-use donation tracking software designed specifically for charities and private schools.

Our Background

DonorChase was founded by Yehuda Neuberger, a certified accountant with extensive experience in the education and charitable sectors. Yehuda spent many years working closely with donors, parents, and other pledgers, during which he recognised the limitations of existing bookkeeping packages. These packages were essential for basic financial information but fell short in managing donor relationships and scheduling follow-ups.

Traditional debt collection software was also unsuitable for charities, as it focused on automated, often aggressive reminders and selling debt to third parties. Such approaches are incompatible with the charitable sector, where maintaining long-term relationships with donors is crucial.

Yehuda's work with constantly updating and sharing spreadsheets led to the creation of DonorChase. Our software provides instant access to underlying bookkeeping data while enabling accurate and timely follow-ups that can be shared across a team. This collaborative approach ensures that all team members, including trustees and volunteers, can stay informed and engaged.

DonorChase is designed specifically for charities and private schools, avoiding terms like 'dunning', 'invoices', and 'balances' that are inappropriate for voluntary donations. Our donation tracking software has been methodically tested and proven to enhance donor engagement and increase donation rates. We invite you to try DonorChase and see the difference it can make for your organisation.

What made us create DonorChase?

After years of assisting charities with donor communications, we identified significant gaps in existing bookkeeping packages. These packages were not designed to handle the unique needs of charities, such as scheduling reminders and sharing information across teams. Traditional debt collection methods were unsuitable for voluntary donations, memberships and school fees. Recognising the need for a tailored solution, we developed DonorChase. Our donation tracking software simplifies donor management, automates reminders, and facilitates collaboration among trustees and volunteers, ensuring that charitable organisations can maintain strong relationships with their donors.
