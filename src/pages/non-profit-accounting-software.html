FAQs

What is the difference between annual and monthly pricing?
Paying annually costs less overall, while paying monthly spreads the cost. You pay by card either way.
How does the charity bookkeeping software sync with my bookkeeping package?
This will happen automatically every evening, and can be done additionally at the click of a button.
Is there a setup fee?
No. Linking the software with your bookkeeping package is straightforward and doesn't take long. The setup in DonorChase is straightforward, and our website tutorial is there to help you. We are also able to help you set up your invoice templates and bookkeeping package, though this support is not included as standard.
What kind of customer support is included?
For your DonorChase non-profit accounting software, we offer a full support site, and support via email or phone as necessary.
What happens if my organisation grows?
Your tier follows the value of your invoices automatically. Each payment is charged at the tier that applies when it's taken, so there are never any charges partway through a period.
