Questions from membership charities

We only record subscriptions when the money arrives. Do we need to change?
DonorChase needs to know what's due so it can show what's outstanding, so subscriptions are raised as invoices in Xero. It's a healthy habit: your trustees can see the full membership income you expect and compare it with what actually arrives. If you'd rather your reports only show money received, run them on a cash basis.
Our treasurer is a volunteer. Is it easy to use?
Yes. It's a single screen of members with what's outstanding, notes and the next follow-up date. There's a help site, and support by email and phone is included.
Will it send reminders to our members automatically?
No. In membership organisations the personal touch matters, so DonorChase reminds your team who to contact and when. You decide how and when to get in touch.
Can we track appeals and memberships separately?
Yes. Multiple dashboards keep ongoing memberships separate from one-off appeals or campaigns, with each member's page showing their full history.
